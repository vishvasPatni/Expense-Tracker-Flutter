A couple of days ago, I introduced the new Expense Tracker app that [Friend's Name/Tag] and I built. Today, let's talk about what's under the hood and the features we are most proud of! 🛠️

When building this, we didn't want just a simple ledger. We wanted a comprehensive financial tool. Here are a few features we implemented to make it stand out:

📊 **Visual Insights:** We used `fl_chart` to build interactive, beautiful charts that give users an immediate grasp of their spending habits versus their budgets.
👯 **Expense Splitting:** Built-in logic to create groups, add members, and split bills dynamically—no more using a separate app just to split dinner costs!
🔒 **Biometric Security:** Personal finance is sensitive. We integrated `local_auth` to provide an app-level biometric/PIN lock.
📶 **Offline Awareness:** Integrated network tracking so the app gracefully handles offline states.

Building the expense splitting logic was definitely a challenging puzzle, but incredibly rewarding to solve.
